Tabular foundation models, from my AI Austria talk

Created on September 30, 2026

2026   ·   tabular-foundation-models   maintenance   ·   talk

TL;DR. Tabular foundation models do for tables what in-context learning does for text. You hand over labelled rows, and one forward pass answers the query rows. There is no training on your data and no hyperparameter search. This post collects the slides, the notebook and the papers from my talk at the AI Austria GenAI Community, Codeforce Edition on 30 September 2026.

What the talk covered

Three parts. First, what a tabular foundation model is and how it differs from a large language model. Second, why prescriptive maintenance needs more than a good prediction, and how a causal foundation model answers “what if” questions while the line stands still. Third, a live notebook in which TabICL and a random forest compete on hand-drawn data and on a maintenance benchmark.

Start here

  • C. Molnar: Tabular Foundation Models, a free online book with runnable examples. The best entry point.
  • N. Hollmann et al.: Accurate predictions on small data with a tabular foundation model, Nature 2025. TabPFN v2, the paper that made the field visible.
  • J. Qu et al.: TabICLv2: A better, faster, scalable, and open tabular foundation model, ICML 2026. The open model used in the notebook.
  • A. Pfefferle et al.: nanoTabPFN, a TabPFN in under 500 lines that you can pre-train yourself in minutes.

How it works

  • S. Müller et al.: Transformers Can Do Bayesian Inference, ICLR 2022. Prior-data fitted networks, the idea behind all of these models.
  • N. Hollmann et al.: TabPFN: A Transformer That Solves Small Tabular Classification Problems in a Second, ICLR 2023. The first tabular foundation model.
  • T. Nagler: Statistical Foundations of Prior-Data Fitted Networks, ICML 2023. Why a forward pass can approximate Bayesian inference.
  • J. Qu et al.: TabICL: A Tabular Foundation Model for In-Context Learning on Large Data, ICML 2025.

Code to play with

  • TabICL: open weights, scikit-learn API, runs on a laptop.
  • TabPFN by Prior Labs, with the TabPFN-3.5 technical report.
  • nanoTabPFN and TFM-Playground: small, readable implementations to learn from.
  • modded-nanoTabPFN: the pre-training speedrun, from 74 minutes to under one minute on one GPU (paper).
  • NVIDIA structured-data-models: one GPU-native API for TabICLv2, Kumo Tabular, TabFM and the relational model KumoRelational, released with the Kumo Tabular announcement.
  • Google TabFM, also available in BigQuery.
  • drawdata and marimo, the tools behind the drawing demo.

Benchmarks

  • TabArena: the living leaderboard for tabular machine learning (paper).

Tabular foundation models for maintenance

  • C. Li et al.: Small data challenges for intelligent prognostics and health management: a review, Artificial Intelligence Review 2024.

From prediction to decision
