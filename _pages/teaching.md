## Fundamentals of Network Theory

**University of Luxembourg, Winter 2026/27** (lecture by Prof. Dr. Thomas Engel, SECAN-Lab)

I design and run the hands-on exercise track for this course. Students work on interactive Python notebooks directly in the browser, nothing to install: each exercise states target values for self-checking, and submissions are autograded.
